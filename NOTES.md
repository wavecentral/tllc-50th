## Open calls

### 1. Nobody wants the Rooms

The Rooms drew **11 purple votes and not one red dot** — the only zone where nobody put a hand
up to own something. It's also the zone that has to run unattended, and three of its ideas need
staffing to work at all:

- **Hire someone to supervise playground for very little ones to play** (PR-05)
- **Kids movie room for those who are tired of being outside** (PR-11)
- **Children's nursery + care** (FU-10)

### 2. Two red dots I may have misread

I called three pale stickers red from colour analysis. FU-02 was one of them and it was
**purple**. The other two:

- **PA-03** *Introduce pastors from past* — photographed nearly as washed out as FU-02's. If
  this is also purple, the red total drops to 9.
- **PA-01** *Former Pastors Speak* — the least washed out of the three, so most likely correct.

### 3. Contested dots still open

| Dots | Between | At stake |
|---|---|---|
| 3 purple | Timeline Game (PA-05) ↔ Computer game w/ timeline questions (PA-06) | Rooms ↔ Ballroom |
| 2 purple | Dream Wall (FU-07) ↔ Stickies to write dreams for future (FU-08) | Rooms ↔ Ballroom |
| 3 purple | Former Pastors Speak (PA-01) ↔ Special Short Speeches from Guests (PA-02) | Ballroom either way |

### 4. Same idea, different zone — left separate on purpose

- **Photo slideshow** — Ballroom (PA-21), Rooms (PR-10) *and* Welcome (PR-17).
- **Ministries video** — a Ballroom cut (PA-04) and a Grounds cut (PA-20).
- **The youth story** — a Ballroom speech (FU-01) and a Welcome storyboard (FU-02).
- **Past pastors** — a Ballroom speech (PA-01) and a Welcome introduction (PA-03).
- **The dream wall** — Rooms (FU-07), Ballroom stickies (FU-08), Ballroom tree (FU-15).

Different paper colour means the committee sited them deliberately. Merge only if you decide
the idea should happen in one place.

### 5. Vote arithmetic doesn't close

10 red dots implies 10 voters, which would put 60 purple dots in play. I count
46. Either people didn't spend all six, or some dots
are hidden under overlapping notes or fell off.

### 6. One loose end
