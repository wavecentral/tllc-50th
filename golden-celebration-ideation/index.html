Narrowing to a shortlist

Top keeps only the highest-voted notes in each column — or, with Grouping on, in each cell of the grid, so you get the best few ideas for the Grounds during the Present rather than the best few across the whole zone. The label next to it says which it is doing. Anything cut is counted at the foot of its stack, so nothing disappears silently.

The column and row totals keep counting every idea, not just the visible ones. Ideas with no votes still take a place in the order, so pair Top with Show: Voted when you want a shortlist with nothing arbitrary in it.
